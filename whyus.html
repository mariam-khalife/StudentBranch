Global Networking Opportunities

Expand Your Professional Circle

Join a global community of 400,000+ members across 160+ countries. Collaborate on research, attend international conferences, and join specialized IEEE societies such as Robotics or Computer Engineering.

How It Works:

  • Use IEEE Collabratec to meet professionals and peers
  • Attend local and international chapter meetings (e.g. IEEE Day, regional TechSummit)
  • Join technical societies by specialty (e.g., Robotics, Computer Engineering)
  • Participate in mentorship and networking programs (e.g., student–industry mentorship sessions)
  • Engage in global IEEE conferences (e.g., IEEE Future Tech Forum or Global AI Congress)
