IEEE Registration Guide

Follow these simple steps to become an IEEE member and unlock a world of opportunities

1

Visit the Official IEEE Website

Start your journey by going to the official IEEE website:

https://www.ieee.org/join
2

Select Student Membership

Once you're on the IEEE website, click the option:

"Join as a student"

This option is designed for students and offers special benefits and pricing.

3

Complete Your Registration

Fill in all the required information:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Other required details

Create a secure password to set up your IEEE account. Be sure to remember it!

Payment Options

You can proceed with payment in one of two ways:

💸 Option 1: Pay in Cash

Visit Zahi Chami’s office and pay the membership fee in cash.

💳 Option 2: Wish Money Transfer
